They say Aphrodite was the most beautiful woman the gods had ever seen, I believe they simply lived to early to meet you, for if the goddess of beauty herself stood before me crowns in gold while you stood beside her wearing nothing but that quiet smile, forgive me my lady but Olympus would lose my attention. Let poets phrase Aphrodite, let kings carve her face into marble, let entire kingdoms kneel and call her divine. I would still search every crowded hall for you. Because Aphrodite may have taught the ancient world what beauty was, but had those poets seen your eyes, heard your laugh or watched the way make an ordinary moment feel sacred. They would have put down their pens. For beauty may have once belonged to the gods.
